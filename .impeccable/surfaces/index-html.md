# Home (/, /en/, /ar/) and shared shell

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Moroccan corporate event and marketing buyers, mostly on phones in daylight. Action: start a quote (wizard or WhatsApp). Proof on hand: services, process, figures, contact facts; photos stay labelled placeholders until the client supplies them. Constraint: the client's graphic charter (Cahier de Prescription Graphique, Nov 2026) and new logo are pinned; no design-guide panel on the public site.

## Direction contract

THESIS: L'Atelier d'Art & d'Ingénierie Graphique. The site is a print run: the logo is painted by brush, the services are inked one plate colour each, the method overprints C, M, Y, K into a finished proof. It refuses the stock hero photo, pill tags and equal card rows.

OWN-WORLD: gallery-white (#FFFFFF) / graphite (#0B0F17) canvas, slate type, 1px hairlines, the logo's five petal inks (yellow #FFC72C, cyan #009EE2, indigo #3D2385, magenta #E6007E, coral #F35338) plus K for accents only; 2px CMYK register line at the page top; Outfit 800/900 display, Plus Jakarta Sans body, Tajawal Arabic, JetBrains Mono tabular figures and dimensions; small square-ish corners, zero pills, metadata as text with · separators; crop marks and overprint (multiply) as the placeholder language.

STORY: the visitor watches the brand being painted, reads "Imprimé, livré, posé.", sees six services as a numbered asymmetric bento each inked in its petal colour, watches a proof come into register across four steps, and starts a quote.

FIRST VIEWPORT: register line, then header (wordmark, five links, language, theme, basket, Devis gratuit). Hero: the full logo large on the white canvas, painted by textured brush strokes (petals fan in base to tip, wordmark swept left to right, CMYK filet drawn, tagline set); beside or below it the H1 offer, sub, Devis gratuit (cyan) and WhatsApp actions, three reassurance lines.

FORM: brief-pinned direction (client charter); a pinned brief beats the roll, so the roll was run for the record only and did not redirect it. seed:122d2a122a1ad5fb. Signature interaction: brush-painted logo on load, then a GSAP ScrollTrigger story (petal inking of services, pinned C→M→Y→K overprint of the method, brush swash under the closing call); reduced motion shows every final state.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
